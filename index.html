07 / COOKING PERFORMANCE

COOKING PERFORMANCE

Engineered for consistent results.

  1. 01

    FACTORY LAB FOOD TEST

    Each program is tested three times (~36 primary tests) to establish the initial setup for further refinement.

  2. 02

    DEMIAND R&D TEAM TEST

    Temperature stability, steam supply and fan speed are validated to ensure the product meets DEMIAND standards.

  3. 03

    CHEF FOOD TEST & FINE-TUNING

    Final adjustments are made by DEMIAND chefs using locally relevant foods and consumer expectations.

  4. 04

    FOCUS GROUP VALIDATION

    Every change is additionally validated with a focus group of 30+ participants to confirm consistent results in different use conditions.
